Proven Success

We’ve built everything from enterprise systems used by thousands to tools that help teams move faster — no job is too big or too small. What sets us apart isn’t just technical expertise — it’s how we think and work. Like a builder who’s also the architect, engineer, and interior designer, we approach every challenge with the full picture in mind, eliminating handoffs and delivering transformative results. Explore the solutions below to see how we’ve delivered results that really matter.

Enterprise Project Management System

Tracks scope, schedule, and funding for tens of thousands of projects worth over $30B. Used daily by hundreds of project stakeholders.

Electronic Statewide Transportation Planning System (e-STIP)

Integrates transportation planning workflows from 26 agencies into a single platform and data source - saving over 20,000 hours per year.

Interactive Mapping

Maps integrated with asset inventories, Google Maps, and ESRI to support planning, project management, and transparency.

Workflow Automation

Eliminated over 20,000 hours per year of redundant data entry through smart form generation, auto-population, replacing manual reports and system integration.

Invoice & Expenditure Tracking

Streamlined invoicing tracking and approval workflows to manage tens of thousands of transactions annually.

Legacy System Modernization

Upgraded entire enterprise systems with modern tech stacks, including server and database migrations, and code upgrade.

Enterprise Dashboards

Real-time dashboards combining data from over 100 database tables and 20 agency offices. Used to drive important decisions related to projects, programs, and overall agency performance.

Why We Started RAPP SOLUTIONS

Because we spent years on the business side trying to fix big problems with tech teams—and we saw the same pattern repeat: broken meetings, endless explanations, slow progress, and expensive solutions that never quite fit. So we built a team inside our agency that worked differently. We partnered directly with business units to uncover the real problems and deliver strategic solutions that truly transformed how people worked—saving over $50 million and eliminating 30,000 hours of manual effort every year.

Now we’re launching a company built on that same proven approach—and the same incredible team. We’re here to help smart, capable teams move forward with clarity, confidence, and tools that make sense.

Our Commitment

We'll treat your business like it’s our own. We’ll care, listen, and stay with you until the solution works just right. Because the right team changes everything — and we’re genuinely excited to be part of yours. We'd love to hear more about your challenges and explore what’s possible.
